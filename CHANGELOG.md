# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Builds for 12 Minecraft versions: 1.20.4, 1.20.6, 1.21.1, 1.21.3, 1.21.4, 1.21.5, 1.21.8, 1.21.10, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3.
- New Foods tab: choose which foods the mod may eat. Common foods first, harmful foods hidden. Golden apples are listed but off by default.
- The mod now picks the best sword in the hotbar and finds food in any hotbar slot.
- Safety stops: low health, sword about to break, full inventory, out of food.
- Menu: sliders, scroll bars, lists no longer jump when you select an entry, status cards, ON/OFF badge, visual hunger bar.
- Whole UI is in English.
- Releases are now published with version tags (`v1.1.0`).

## 1.0.0
- First version: attack selected mobs, eat when hungry, pick up drops. Mob menu with search.
